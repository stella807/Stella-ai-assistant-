What it does

Built for 1am, not for a demo.

Adaptive check-ins

Every 60 minutes early on, tightening toward 15 as the night picks up — and again after a miss. A missed check-in alerts whoever is watching, right then.

Someone watching

Invite a guardian with a six-character code. Alerts reach their phone even with the app closed — and nothing on a lock screen ever shows a location.

Honest drink log

Tap from the venue's likely menu. Everything resolves to standard drinks, so a craft IPA counts as 1.8, not 1. The estimate is shown as a range, never a verdict.

Getting home

Real Uber quotes and booking where it's configured, a one-tap hand-off to Uber or Lyft where it isn't. The app always tells you which one it's doing.

SOS & medical escalation

Hold-to-send SOS with a silent mode. A red-flag checklist, the right emergency number for where you are, and a script for the dispatcher. Never behind a paywall.

Pharmacy run

Water, electrolytes and real food, lined up when the night gets away from someone — authorized while sober, with a hard cap. Never alcohol.

Crew

Start a crew at the table and see who's getting ahead and who's gone quiet. Counts and check-ins only — never anyone's location.

Personal concierge

A vetted, insured professional for one in-person task — grab something, sit with a friend — at a spend cap you set. Live in Puerto Rico, Texas and Los Angeles.

Games that reward getting home

Seven crew games. Every one scores on checking in, pacing, water or getting home. None score on how much anyone drank.

How it works

Four steps. One of them is getting home.

  1. 1

    Start your night

    Log a drink or two — the app paces its check-ins to how the night is actually going.

  2. 2

    Pick someone to watch

    They see your check-ins and get alerted the moment one is missed, not the next morning.

  3. 3

    Get real help

    A ride home, supplies delivered, or a vetted assistant sent to you in person at a spend cap you set.

  4. 4

    Get home

    Sharing ends on its own when you're home safe — nothing keeps running after it should.

Where we said no

Some features are a promise not to build them.

No covert tracking

Only the person being located can share it. Every grant expires, stays visible to them, ends when they get home, and can be revoked at any time.

No charging a drunk person's card

Purchases are authorized while sober with a hard cap. A late-night order is queued and asked about later — a confirmation taken while impaired is refused.

No coaching to hide being drunk

Nothing speeds up sobering up. We ship the honest version: hydration, food, pacing, a real clock — and a list of the myths.

No invented fares

Where we can't quote or book for real, we hand off to the provider's own app. A made-up price is the worst thing to show someone deciding whether to drive.

Safehubby is not an emergency service and never tells anyone they are safe to drive. In an emergency, call your local emergency number first.
