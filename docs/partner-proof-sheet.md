# Safehubby: hoja para referidos / referral sheet

For care homes, home-care agencies and hospital social workers in Puerto Rico. One page, Spanish first, English below.

**Before using it:** fill in every line marked ✏️ with the truth, or delete the line. Never leave a promise on it you cannot prove if they ask. Lines without ✏️ are already true from what safehubby.com says today.

---

## Español

### Qué es Safehubby

Un servicio en Puerto Rico que ayuda a familias, sobre todo a las que viven fuera, a cuidar de un familiar mayor en la isla. Lo maneja una persona de aquí.

### Lo que hacemos

- Acompañamos a citas y estadías en el hospital, esperamos con el paciente y mantenemos a la familia al tanto.
- Farmacia, compra y recogidos, con un límite de gasto que pone la familia.
- Transporte de ida y vuelta, con el precio a la vista primero.

### Lo que NO hacemos

- No damos cuidado médico ni de enfermería.
- No damos medicamentos ni tomamos decisiones de salud.
- No reemplazamos a su equipo ni a su agencia: cubrimos lo que queda entre una cita y otra.
- En una emergencia, se llama primero al 911.

### Cómo protegemos al paciente y a la familia

- **La familia escoge quién va** y ve la foto de la persona antes de que llegue. Nadie llega sin anunciarse.
- **La familia pone el límite de gasto.** Nunca se cobra más de eso.
- **Check-ins y SOS siempre gratis.**
- **No guardamos códigos de puerta ni llaves con la dirección** del paciente.

## English

### What Safehubby is

A service in Puerto Rico that helps families, especially those living off the island, look after an older relative here. Run by a local person.

### What we do

- Go with patients to appointments and hospital stays, wait with them, and keep the family updated.
- Pharmacy runs, groceries and pickups, with a spending limit the family sets.
- Rides there and back, with the price shown first.

### What we do NOT do

- No medical or nursing care.
- We do not give medication or make health decisions.
- We do not replace your team or agency: we cover what falls between appointments.
- In an emergency, call 911 first.

### How we protect the patient and the family

- **The family chooses who goes** and sees that person's photo before they arrive. Nobody shows up unannounced.
- **The family sets the spending limit.** We never charge past it.
- **Check-ins and SOS are always free.**
- **Door codes and keys are never kept with the patient's address.**

## Notes for you (do not hand this part out)

- The three ✏️ lines under "how we protect" are what a care home or hospital will ask about first. If you do not screen helpers yet, the cheapest strong step is a criminal background certificate (*Certificado de Antecedentes Penales*), which anyone in Puerto Rico can request from the Police online. Asking every helper for one, and keeping a copy, lets you write that line truthfully.
- If you have no insurance, say so honestly if asked, and consider a basic general liability policy: it is often the first thing a facility asks for.
- Print it on one sheet, both sides (Spanish on the front). Bring it to visits, attach it as a PDF to the follow-up email.
